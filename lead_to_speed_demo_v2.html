The problem

Leads don't care whether the office is busy.

A viewing request, valuation enquiry or simple question can arrive while agents are on calls, in viewings, away from their desks or dealing with the next lead. Lead-to-Speed is designed to make the follow-through visible and accountable.

03

Without a check, a valuable enquiry can sit unattended until someone eventually finds it.

Behind the scenes

Automation supports the agent. It doesn't replace them.

The interactive experience is a simulation of the current workflow logic: capture the lead, notify the team, check for action, remind when necessary and escalate an unattended lead.

The exact integrations and timing can be tailored during implementation. The demo focuses on the workflow logic and the business outcome.
